# TallyPrime feature parity

> **Parity is measured against 381 audited TallyPrime features and NOTHING ELSE.**
> Capabilities Tally does not have — ghost recall, undo, bulk paste entry, the open
> API, CRM, HR, custom fields, Mac/browser/sync, the operator model, the sync mismatch
> report, the Comsky-vs-Tally checker, saved report views, the Reports Center, the universal
> quick-create, shared views and row filters — live in `parity-data.json`
> under `beyond` and are deliberately excluded from the percentage. Folding them in would
> flatter the score while hiding the real gaps; keeping them out means this number
> answers exactly one question: *what would a switcher miss?*
>
> Last audit refresh: 2026-10-22 (Phase 29, parity wave 3: 15 rows re-judged, see below); Phases 25 to 28 and Phase 30 moved no row (they are `beyond`
> work, and Phase 26 only rewrote the notes of the two filter rows; Phase 30 only the note of `tdl-customization`). Phases 18-23 moved this number only by
> corrections; Phase 24 was the first parity wave since Phase 17 and Phase 29 is the second. The rules it was scored by:
> a status is never higher than the builder's own report, `covered` needs the builder AND the
> review/fix pass to agree, and anything not yet compared with a live TallyPrime, the GST portal
> or the IRP says so in its row. What is still open, with the reason and the next step for each
> item, is in `docs/research/ROADMAP-ADDENDUM.md`.
