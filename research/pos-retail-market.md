> **Status:** research and design input only. Nothing here is built, no gate (assert, shots, build, perf) was run, and no file other than this one was written in the repo. ## 0. Read this first

**If you read nothing else.** (1) The floor of an Indian counter POS is a shift with a cash close, hold and recall, linked returns, schemes, loyalty, role-limited approvals and device support (silent thermal print, drawer, scanner, scale, terminal); Comsky has the accounting engine under all of that and none of the counter. (2) The loudest complaints about the incumbents are support after the sale, renewals and licence locks, and the price of changing a rule, not missing features. (3) Four statutory traps bite a naive POS: the apparel and footwear rate that depends on the unit price, MRP-inclusive pricing with no sale above MRP, discounts and schemes that must be allocated before tax, and invoice numbers that must be consecutive. (4) Build the counter as its own root and writer on the same `Voucher`; ship v1 as a single-store, one-to-three-counter Windows desktop product; keep cashier controls detective until the server enforces rights. (5) Pilot with two or three of the owner's own Tally retail customers, and have a CA sign section 6 first.

### 0.1 What I could not verify, and why it matters

1. **No live account, no hardware, no customer interview.** I used no product. Behaviour comes from vendor pages, help articles, release notes and reviews. A feature a page does not mention is "not found", not "absent". Speed claims ("2x faster", "40% less billing time") are vendors' own and none states a method.
2. **Walls.** Lightspeed's site and help centre returned 403 to every fetch, so Lightspeed rests on search summaries **[Ws]**. Shopify's help articles returned 403 to a direct download and were index pages to the fetch tool; Shopify's pricing and offline pages were read, its cash-tracking and staff pages only as search summaries. Capterra, G2, GetApp and the Play Store were not readable. RetailGraph's own site has a broken certificate and could not be read. Khatabook's page came back empty. Pine Labs' and Razorpay's developer pages are overviews, so **I did not read how an amount is pushed to a terminal and how the result comes back**. Square's help centre redirected one article elsewhere.
3. **Review evidence is thin and self-selected.** Trustpilot pages say the company has not invited reviews; SoftwareSuggest showed 3 of 125 Vyapar reviews; the App Store page for Zoho POS had a 4.6 rating from 31 people and no text. GoFrugal's Trustpilot score (1.5) does not reconcile with its own star split (82% five-star), so I use its complaints and not its score. No Reddit, no YouTube. **I found no independent complaint about TallyPrime's POS mode at all**; every critical statement about it comes from a competitor (Ginesys) or from Tally's own help text.
4. **Prices conflict** between a vendor's page, a reseller listing and an aggregator (Vyapar, Marg, Odoo, Zoho). I give each with its source and do not pick (Appendix B).
5. **Vendor scale numbers are marketing and are counted differently** ("1.5 crore businesses", "1 million businesses", "6 lakh businesses", "2,15,000 users"). I quote them only as claims.
6. **Statute: what I read and what I did not.** Read in full: the items listed under Method above. **Not read by me as a primary text (the sibling study `pos-payments-hardware-compliance.md` read some of these, and I say where I lean on it):** the e-invoice threshold notification (secondary summaries and one GSTN advisory), the dynamic-QR notifications (a TaxGuru summary), Rule 46A, the composition-scheme rules (secondary), RBI's card-on-file circular (search summaries), the DPDP Rules 2025 (search summaries), Meta's INR rate sheet (third-party figures), the cash-receipt section of the Income-tax Act (search summaries, and sources disagree on its number in the 2025 Act), the TRAI DLT rules (provider summaries). **The Act copy I read is of 1 Aug 2021**, so later amendments are not in it. And **what "sale value" means in the apparel and footwear threshold (Rs 2,500 per piece) is not defined in the press release or FAQ I read**: section 6 shows what secondary sources say and marks it for a CA.
7. **This is not legal advice** and several rows of section 6 are my reading of a rule. Section 11 lists what a practising CA must sign.

### 0.2 Twelve findings that change what we build

1. **Every Indian counter-POS pitch uses the same five headlines, and the floor behind them is higher than "bill and print".** Marg, Vyapar, GoFrugal and BUSY all lead with fast barcode billing, several payment modes, thermal printing, WhatsApp or SMS bills and (except BUSY) loyalty [W MG-retail, VY-home, GF-retail, BU-pos]. GoFrugal's own knowledge-base table of what its counter products do runs to 66 rows, among them session, hold and recall, sales return, cash-drawer integration, a continuous invoice sequence, allow-negative-sales, open items, price levels, free quantity, tax-slab support, salesman commission, weighable barcodes, item favourites, pole display, delivery note, matrix items and serialised items [W GF-kb]. A "POS mode" that stops at the Sales voucher with a thermal print will lose to the cheapest of these.
2. **A per-register session (opening float, cash in and out with reasons, counted against expected, a difference that may need approval, a printed summary) is present in every strong product I could read and absent in Comsky.** Odoo, Zoho ERP's retail channel, Shopify, GoFrugal ("Session" in all four of its products, "Cashier shift management" only in two) and LogicERP (at its Enterprise price) all have it [W OD-use, ZE-sess, GF-kb, LG-desktop; Ws Shopify]. Comsky has no shift, float, over/short ledger or Z report [R pos-code-audit §2.4].
3. **The complaints that recur are about money and support, not features.** After-sales support, renewals and licence locks, and the cost of custom changes are the themes across Marg, GoFrugal, BUSY and Vyapar reviews, dated 2025 and 2026 [W MG-tp, GF-tp, BU-tp, VY-tp]. One Vyapar reviewer does name the counter: bottlenecks for fast retail counters, no keyboard shortcuts, a WhatsApp invoice sent before the payment is recorded [W VY-tp, 14 Mar 2026]. **[J]** Published pricing, nothing important behind an add-on, support that the reseller channel can give, and self-diagnosis in the app are positioning, not polish.
4. **TallyPrime's POS is a Sales voucher type with a switch, not a counter system.** Its help describes "Use for POS invoicing", a party that can be "Not Applicable", cash tendered and balance, multi-mode payment (cash, card, cheque, gift voucher), a barcode scanned into the Alias field, a POS Register, and buy-one-get-one through separate Actual and Billed quantity columns; it says barcodes **cannot be printed as labels**; it documents no shift, loyalty, customer display or hold, and Tally's own marketing page does not claim them [W TL-help, TL-mkt, TL-small]. So "weak" is a fair word for the counter, and not for the books. **The real competitor of a Tally retailer's setup is "a POS plus Tally", not "a POS instead of Tally"**: GoFrugal sells a Tally integration add-on [W GF-price], and a Razorpay integrator and Oliver POS's connector page advertise posting sales to Tally [Ws]. Comsky's opening is books and POS in one box, with Tally XML in and out so the CA does not lose anything [C CLAUDE.md, Tally import and Export Data].
5. **The market is consolidating and tiering.** GoFrugal's CEO letter says it is now part of Zoho (the letter is undated; one directory lists 1 July 2023, a Petpooja blog says February 2025, I did not settle it) [W GF-ceo; Ws], and Zoho also sells its own POS, which is native to Zoho Books [W ZP-what]. LogicERP puts shift, multi-location and gift vouchers in higher editions (Enterprise Basic is ₹6,154 a month against ₹608 for the lowest) [W LG-desktop]; GoFrugal prices per store with add-ons for loyalty, scale support and Tally [W GF-price]. **[J]** A single published price with every counter feature included, per company and not per terminal, is a real option the leaders are not taking. A Zoho-backed stack is the strongest "books plus POS" rival.
6. **"Offline" means four different things, and India's two main tenders need the network.** Odoo keeps carts and orders but not invoices, new customers or payment terminals [W OD-use]; Shopify offers cash and manual tenders offline and cards only if switched on [W SH-off]; Lightspeed treats offline as a short backup after a two-minute drop and needs a prior login [Ws]; Square's offline cards must be uploaded within 72 hours and the merchant carries any decline [Ws]. UPI and EMI cannot be confirmed offline at all. **[J]** Offline for Comsky means: the cart, stock, numbering and cash and "recorded tender" work with no network; UPI and card are typed references reconciled later against the bank statement the app already imports [C `core/bankStatement.ts`, `core/brsMatch.ts`].
7. **Five statutory traps break a naive POS, and one of them is not modelled at all.** (a) From 22 Sep 2025 apparel and footwear are taxed at 5% up to Rs 2,500 per piece or pair and 18% above, so the rate depends on the unit price [W PIB56]; Comsky's rate engine is dated by day but has no value rule [C `src/core/gstRates.ts`; a grep of `src/core` for the threshold finds nothing]. (b) A packaged good may not be sold above its MRP, and MRP includes all taxes [W LM]. (c) A discount shown on the invoice leaves the taxable value; buy-one-get-one is two supplies at one price; free gifts are not a supply and block input credit [W CGST-act s.15(3), C92]. (d) Invoice numbers are consecutive, at most 16 characters, unique in the financial year; a consolidated end-of-day invoice is allowed only below Rs 200, to an unregistered buyer who does not ask for an invoice [W R46, CGST-act s.31(3)(b)]. (e) HSN digits depend on turnover: 4 up to Rs 5 crore (and optional on bills to unregistered buyers), 6 above [W N78].
8. **Cashier theft is detective, not preventive, until the server enforces rights, and a new fraud sits at the tender.** The code audit's position stands: rights are client-side and the server accepts any write from any member [R pos-code-audit §9]. The market's controls are approvals (Odoo's three employee tiers; a Zoho session that can need approval to close; Marg's per-user cash-drawer permissions; GoFrugal's cap on cash discount) [W OD-emp, ZE-sess, MG-pos, GF-rel]. The Indian addition is **fake UPI confirmations** (spoofed apps, played-back soundbox tones, edited screenshots) [Ws razorpay-learn, deccanchronicle], which is why a typed reference plus a bank-statement match matters more than a "paid" button.
9. **Payments are an ecosystem to plug into, not rebuild.** Pine Labs sells a "POS API suite" and a Hub terminal that does billing, inventory and khata [W PL-dev, PL-hub]; Razorpay cites 200+ billing partners [W RZ-docs]; Odoo ships certified modules for Pine Labs (India only, four credentials, India-hosted proxy) and Razorpay [W OD-pine, OD-rzp]; LogicERP lists Paytm, PayU, Pine Labs and Razorpay [W LG-home]; Marg reconciles UPI, card and net-banking receipts to bills across 140+ banks [W MG-pos]. **[J]** v1 records tender and reference; v2 integrates one terminal partner chosen by a partner conversation, not by a guess. Two facts from the sibling payments study change the economics and the design: NPCI's FAQ of 15 Sep 2026 introduces a **0.4% merchant discount on person-to-merchant UPI payments above Rs 2,000** (capped at Rs 300 from Rs 75,000; none up to Rs 2,000 or for small merchants taking up to Rs 1 lakh a month on a QR into their own accounts), which **merchants may not pass on to customers**, with the start date unverified (one page says it moved to after Diwali); and a UPI tender should carry a **confirmation state** (confirmed by a PSP, a terminal or the bank statement, or attested by the cashier) with a list of the unconfirmed ones at shift close [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §0 findings 1 and 3].
10. **A WhatsApp bill is two different products.** The free `wa.me` link the app already builds opens the person's own WhatsApp with typed text and cannot attach a file [R pos-code-audit §8]. A real e-bill (a PDF or hosted link sent from the shop's number) needs a Business Platform account through a provider, an approved template and a per-message fee: Meta has charged per message since 1 July 2025, utility messages inside the service window were free from then and are charged from 1 October 2026 according to its page, and a third party puts the India utility rate near ₹0.115 [W WA-meta; Ws]. (The sibling study found a Meta product page that still calls an in-window utility reply free and says the two statements conflict, so treat it as chargeable until confirmed [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §4.3].) SMS bills need the retailer's TRAI DLT registration and an approved template [Ws]. Marg and Vyapar advertise sending at the time of billing without saying how [W MG-pos, VY-home].
11. **What Comsky has against that floor is an engine, not a counter.** Every statutory report reads a Sales voucher correctly; the Sales screen takes 115 key actions for a 10-line invoice and asks four questions before the first item; there is no tender list, time, terminal, cashier, shift, barcode field (alias only), MRP-inclusive price, bill-level discount, hold, loyalty, receipt printer path or customer table [R pos-code-audit §0 findings 2, 3, 9, 11, 12]. What is already in `main` and not wired: scan-burst detection, exact-code resolution, GS1 and scale-label parsing, label layout and barcode encoders as pure files [C `src/core/scanInput.ts` 860 lines, `scanResolve.ts`, `scale.ts`, `barcode/gs1.ts`, `labelLayout.ts`; no import from `src/ui`].
12. **The owner's channel is an advantage no vendor above has.** The owner works at a Tally reseller and partner [C CLAUDE.md]: an installed base of Tally retail customers who already have a CA, an implementation team, and (unlike a software-only start-up) a way to give the local support that is the top complaint about Marg and GoFrugal. **[J]** v1 should be shaped for a **small multi-counter retailer with a Tally-using CA**, not for a kirana first and not for a chain.

## 2. Product notes: what each does, what users say, what to copy and avoid

Each note ends with **Copy** and **Avoid** lines, which are my judgement **[J]**. Where a point comes from a sibling doc it says so.

### 2.1 Vyapar

- **What it does [W VY-home, VY-pos].** Counter billing with item or whole-bill discounts and several tax rates on one bill; **MRP-based billing with the tax inside the MRP**; estimates that convert to invoices, delivery challans, order tracking; a barcode generator and scanner (USB and Bluetooth), with generating and importing items from barcodes on its Retail Pro plan; thermal printers at 2, 3 and 4 inches by Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or cable; a cash drawer that opens on a cash sale; weighing machines (Retail Pro); loyalty rules with points earned and redeemed; e-invoice and e-way bill; WhatsApp, SMS and PDF sharing; role logins; offline with sync; multi-counter sync and several outlets on one dashboard (a Gold-plan multi-device sync is named). Not found on the pages: hold and recall, shift or day close, customer display, how a split tender works, how a return works.
- **What users say [W VY-tp, VY-ss].** Trustpilot: 3.6 from 31 reviews (the company has not invited reviews). Praise: easy for non-technical staff, offline mode, bill sharing, e-invoicing, desktop and mobile at no extra cost (Dec 2025 to Apr 2026). Complaints: one 1-star (14 Mar 2026) calls it good basic software with "terrible workflow bottlenecks for fast retail counters" and names no keyboard shortcuts, a WhatsApp invoice sent before payment is recorded, duplicate customer names refused, and difficulty deleting duplicate items; one 2-star (11 Mar 2026) wants custom roles instead of predefined ones; 1-stars (23 and 30 Jul 2026) about a misleading discount ad and slow replies after payment. SoftwareSuggest (3 of 125 reviews shown, Jul 2024 to Jan 2026): limited user licences, thin for complex or specialised businesses, no marketplace integrations.
- **Copy.** Breadth at one low price; MRP-inclusive billing; the drawer opening on a cash tender; one product on desktop and mobile. **Avoid.** Sending a bill message before the payment is recorded (the receipt must exist only after the tender); predefined-only roles; refusing a second customer with the same name (two "Ramesh" are normal).

### 2.2 GoFrugal

- **Names.** The pages I read name RPOS6, RPOS7, TRUEPOS, GoBill (mobile and Windows), GoSure, OrderEasy and "Retail ERP". **RetailEasy and Retail360, which the owner's list names, did not appear on any GoFrugal page I read**; one search summary describes RetailEasy as a modular ERP and POS suite for retailers, hospitality and distributors (cloud, on-premise or hybrid, with batch and expiry), and a search for Retail360 found nothing [W GF-home, GF-retail, GF-kb; Ws].
- **What it does [W GF-kb, GF-retail, GF-pos, GF-hold, GF-price].** The KB table (66 rows, four products) is the best statement of a mature Indian counter: sessions, sales return, **hold and recall** (a held bill is recalled only at the counter that held it; the bill has a separate "delivery bill" mode that needs a customer and address), cash-drawer integration, a continuous invoice sequence, allow-negative-sales and offline live stock, quick add customer, bill and item discount, price level, free quantity, tax-slab support, salesman commission, weighable-barcode and scale integration, **open item** (a one-off item sold without a master), item images and favourites for touch, loyalty, offer, delivery note, sales order, pole display, email invoice, year-end processing, reprint and session-summary print. **Not in all four products:** loyalty, delivery note and unique-barcode support are missing from GoBill mobile, shift management is missing from RPOS6 and TRUEPOS, the pole display is missing from TRUEPOS and GoBill, and serialised and matrix items are not in RPOS6. The site adds GoBill mobile billing to cut queues at peak hours, a branded customer app, ONDC, Unicommerce and Shopify integrations, and location-wise selling prices with per-store GST structures. A 2017 release note (RPOS 6.5.8.8 SP22, 18 Aug 2017) shows how long some rules have existed: customer-type discounts by amount slab and date (not offline), a configurable cap on cash discount, a security code on stock transfer in, HQ approval on wastage [W GF-rel]. The pricing page lists "offers and promotions", "gift coupons" and "sales commissions" as **coming soon** for its cloud product [W GF-price].
- **What users say [W GF-tp].** Trustpilot (score not reconcilable with its split): slow or missing support (3 Feb 2024, 20 Mar 2026, 5 May 2026); a quote of about ₹8,000 a day per person for a small tender-rule change (5 May 2026); an e-way bill update described as non-compliant and three months in development (20 Mar 2026); a supermarket user without proper training (14 Jan 2025). Praise is from 2022 and 2023 (supermarket and FMCG use, support responsiveness). Capterra summaries (2018, 2021) praise price and ease and criticise after-sales support [Ws].
- **Copy.** The whole KB list as a checklist: session, hold and recall, sales return, continuous invoice sequence, allow negative, open item, item favourites, free quantity, tax-slab support, weighable barcode, pole display. Per-store price with named add-ons. **Avoid.** Selling a tender-rule change by the day; feature gaps between its own products; paid add-ons for basics.

### 2.3 BUSY

The retail page shows buy-one-get-one and "20% off MRP" style schemes, batch and expiry, multi-store stock transfer and consolidated reports. A comparison article lists held transactions [Ws]. Not found: hold in the POS page, loyalty beyond "loyalty discounts", touch, shortcuts beyond F4, customer display, drawer, shift, offline.

POS billing praised in January and February 2022.
- **Copy.** A series per user (Comsky's equivalent is a series per terminal); the "Invalid Item" message; the date field that may be fixed. **Avoid.** A licence check that can stop a counter mid-day.

### 2.4 Marg (retail and supermarket)

- **What it does [W MG-retail, MG-pos].** Add items by name, code or barcode; discounts and several payment modes (cash, UPI, card) in one bill; thermal or regular printers; **hold a bill and serve the next customer**; barcode labels with SKU, serial or batch; live stock after each sale; loyalty points and welcome offers for first-time buyers; home delivery and online orders (pending, packed, delivered); stock comparison and transfer between stores; slab-wise discounts and schemes; **user-specific cash-drawer permissions**; bills to the customer's WhatsApp at the time of billing, SMS and email; touch-screen and Android terminals; invoices in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and other languages; **UPI, card and net-banking receipts matched to bills across 140+ banks** (MargPay); rack-wise stock; e-invoice from the bill screen. Pharma specifics are in `pos-pharma-market.md`.
- **What users say [W MG-tp].** Trustpilot 2.3 from 116 reviews (20 in the last year; the company has not replied to negative ones): unanswered calls after payment (May and Aug 2026), issues pushed to dealers who do not answer (5 Aug and 17 Sep 2026), a ticket closed unresolved after a month (20 Apr 2026), "stock changed by the software" (21 Jun 2026), half-page prints after a renewal and update (2 to 3 Jun 2026), renewals called overpriced (1 Nov 2025). Praise: a retail owner of ten years calls it reliable (17 Dec 2025). Marg's own help centre explains "database damage" after a power cut or an unclosed program [R pos-pharma-market §2.2, source S7], which matters for Indian power conditions.
- **Copy.** Bank-statement matching of UPI and card to the bill; a welcome offer; multilingual receipts; a local support network. **Avoid.** Support that depends on a dealer's goodwill; a store that a power cut can corrupt (Comsky's IndexedDB is transactional [C `src/data/db.ts`], and the cart draft must be too).

### 2.5 LogicERP

- **What it does [W LG-home, LG-pos, LG-kb, LG-desktop, LG-pine].** The POS knowledge-base lists ten titles: configurable POS, multiple views, touch and mobile POS, flexible schemes and promotions, easy exchange and return management, an **integrated alteration module** (apparel), item images at the POS, a **cashier and shift system**, multiple payment modes, and integrations with Paytm, PayU, Mobikwik, Pine Labs and Jio Money. The module page adds loyalty points with **OTP-based redemption**, membership packages by brand or category, discount coupons with OTP redemption, gift vouchers, scale integration, expiry prompts, UPC scanning, unit conversion, online ordering and home-delivery apps. The Pine Labs page says the bill amount goes to the terminal so the cashier does not retype it and the confirmation comes back to the ERP for end-of-day reconciliation. **Features are tiered by edition** (monthly, billed yearly): multiple barcodes per item and multiple price lists at ₹1,008; shade and size matrix, gift vouchers and coupons and third-party loyalty at ₹1,167; shift-manager controls at ₹1,512; multi-location, cashier and shift management at Enterprise Basic ₹6,154.
- **What users say.** No Trustpilot reviews exist [W LG-tp]; I found no independent review. Testimonials on its own site are apparel chains (50, 100 and 500+ terminals), so it is strongest where there are many terminals [W LG-home].
- **Copy.** OTP before a loyalty or coupon redemption (a cheap fraud and consent control); the shade and size matrix; an alteration flow for apparel; the amount pushed to the card terminal. **Avoid.** Putting shift and multi-location behind a four-fold price step (₹1,512 to ₹6,154 a month).

### 2.6 RetailGraph and Ginesys

- **RetailGraph [Ws].** Positioned for small and medium retailers and wholesalers; desktop, Android and web; garment, supermarket, pharma and FMCG formats; .NET with SQL Server. Its site could not be read and I found no vendor-confirmed feature list, so it carries almost no weight here.
- **Ginesys [W GIN].** A retail-chain ERP (apparel in particular). I use its blog only as a **competitor's claims about Tally** (11 Apr 2025: accounting-first, delayed stock sync, no unified multi-outlet view, no integrated POS, no omnichannel sync, slows as volume grows) and about Shopify POS in India (no native GST invoice, no native UPI, weak offline) [Ws]. It is biased, and none of it is evidence of how Tally behaves.

### 2.7 TallyPrime's own POS mode

- **What the help documents [W TL-help].** A Sales voucher type with "Use for POS invoicing" set to Yes (the POS Register needs it); a buyer that can be "Not Applicable"; sales ledger and stock items, quantity 1 by default; the rate editable only if an F12 option is on; cash is the default mode with tendered cash and balance; **multi-mode payment** (Alt+I) with gift vouchers (ledgers under Sundry Debtors or Creditors), cards and cheques (bank ledgers) and cash; a **voucher class** that pre-defines the payment ledgers; barcode scanned into the item name field and matched on the **Alias** (a 16-digit number is the page's example); "barcodes cannot be printed as labels"; Alt+P prints on a chosen POS printer and format; a **POS Register** that drills into invoices and can show estimated gross profit; discounts, free items, offers (same item through Actual and Billed quantity columns, different items through an "allow zero-valued transactions" setting); expired and zero-balance batches selectable by F12; voucher numbers over 16 characters are not exported to e-return formats.
- **What Tally says about itself [W TL-mkt, TL-small].** Date and time stamps and an audit trail, several discount types, change due from the amount tendered, price levels for retail, wholesale or loyalty segments, godowns for a second outlet, hold implied for repair and service shops. **Not claimed:** loyalty points, shifts, customer display, touch screen, a hardware list, offline use, multi-store POS.
- **What I take from it [J].** It documents everything a Tally retailer already has and, by what it leaves out, most of what this study lists as missing: shift and cash close, loyalty, display, a hold you can rely on, label printing, thermal receipts, a speed path. It is also the proof of the Alias barcode convention the migration must honour (section 4.2).

### 2.8 Zoho POS (and Zoho ERP's retail channel)

- **What it does [W ZP-feat, ZP-what, ZP-home, ZP-ios, ZP-news, ZE-sess].** Windows, Android and iOS apps; offline billing that syncs later; hold and recall; discounts; cash, card and split payments; **session and cash tracking**; multiple registers by plan; barcode generation and scanning (camera or scanner); weighing machine, customer display, pole display, cash drawer; WhatsApp integration (credits sold as an add-on); price lists; transfer orders and per-location pricing; returns, credit notes and refunds; GST through Zoho Books; unit-of-measure conversion (February 2026, Windows and web only for now); interface in 13 languages; 4.6 on the App Store from 31 ratings. A Zoho ERP help page on retail sessions says closing a session shows expected against counted cash, lets a cash-in or cash-out correct a mismatch first, and **may require approval depending on settings**; closing interrupts a sale in progress on that register. The App Store release notes (dated without a year; the newest entry is from October) show a cadence of bug fixes plus Pine Labs transaction cancellation, register-based item restrictions and location-based tax rules, and "preparing for" multiple selling prices and MRPs per batch or lot.
- **What it lacks on the pages [W ZP-feat].** Exchange, coupons, gift cards, e-invoice and a list of which features sit in which plan; the India page quotes dollars.
- **Zoho Inventory and Zoho Books.** I found no POS screen in either; a Petpooja blog (a competitor) ranks Zoho Books among India's retail POS options as the one for accounting-heavy businesses [Ws], and Zoho's own pages put the counter in the separate Zoho POS product, which connects to Zoho Books for the ledger and GST [W ZP-what].
- **Copy.** The approval-on-close setting; register-based item restrictions; the UoM conversion (sell trays as pieces). **Avoid.** Prices in dollars on an Indian page; a feature table that does not say which plan has what.

### 2.9 Odoo POS (the best-documented behaviour, read in full)

- **Session control [W OD-use].** Open register with a confirmed opening cash; hamburger menu **Cash In/Out with a reason** (basic or advanced employees only); **Close Register** shows orders and expected amounts by payment method, takes a coin and note count, and if counted differs a "Payments Difference" window appears; **"Set Maximum Difference"** stops a cashier closing above a limit and tells them to ask a manager; the register can be closed only by advanced employees; a restaurant register cannot close with draft orders.
- **Employees [W OD-emp].** Login by badge scan, name pick or PIN (a PIN is still asked after a badge); three tiers: minimal (sell, assign a customer, apply a promo code), basic (open the register, cash in/out, create customers, refunds, cancel orders, manual discounts and price changes, switch price list, apply loyalty rewards), advanced (create products, back office, close the register).
- **Pricing [W OD-pricing, OD-shop].** Global and line discounts; promotions apply automatically on a minimum spend or product; codes and coupons typed by the cashier and printed on receipts; **buy X get Y rewards must be added manually by the cashier**; loyalty cards need a customer first; gift cards sold at the register; an eWallet for store credit; **discount tags** (scan a special barcode to take a percentage off the last item, suited to near-expiry) and a **barcode nomenclature** that encodes weight, price or discount; quotations and sales orders loaded into the register with a down payment; "ship later".
- **Returns [W OD-use].** Order-based refunds (pick items and quantities from a paid order) and standalone refunds; the refund can go to a payment method or be issued as a **gift card**.
- **Orders in progress [W OD-use].** A plus sign sets the current order aside and starts another; the Orders button lists past and ongoing orders.
- **Offline [W OD-use].** Products and customers are loaded into the browser cache at session start (an operator setting caps how many; customers with the most orders and favourite products load first); orders created offline wait in the cache; **do not close or reload the browser until synced**; not available offline: generating invoices (made after reconnect), registering customers, detailed product information, real-time stock sync, payment terminals; printers and scales on the local network keep working.
- **Hardware and payments [W OD-pos, OD-pine, OD-rzp].** Receipt printers, customer display, scale, electronic shelf labels through an IoT connection; payment terminals including **Pine Labs (Indian companies only; four credentials; an India-hosted proxy if the database is elsewhere)** and **Razorpay (an API key, a username and the device serial; the device needs 10% battery)**.
- **Price [W OD-price].** POS is free as the single app on Odoo Online.
- **Copy.** Almost all of it, in particular: the three employee tiers, the maximum-difference rule with manager approval, cash-in/out with a reason, refund-to-gift-card, discount tags and the weight/price barcode nomenclature, the offline cache size setting. **Avoid.** A buy-X-get-Y that the cashier must add by hand (slow and a fraud surface); "do not reload the browser" as the offline rule; deferring invoices while offline (a B2B customer needs the invoice then).

### 2.10 Shopify POS, Lightspeed Retail, Square for Retail

- **Shopify [W SH-price, SH-off; Ws].** In rupees: plans from ₹1,994 a month and **POS Pro at ₹7,000 a month per location**; staff roles and permissions, returns and exchanges at any location, faster discount and stock-transfer flows and cross-store analytics are Pro. Offline checkout is cash and manual tenders for everyone; card payments offline need a setting, a staff permission and (per a help summary) a transaction and device limit. Cash tracking sessions exist, can be closed by staff or remotely, and (April 2026, third-party reports) now use a drawer model shared across devices with a categorised reason for every non-sale cash movement; the API data for them is stated for POS Pro locations [Ws]. An Indian merchant on the app store (March 2026) reports disconnects from the card reader and printer and re-login every few days [Ws].
- **Lightspeed [Ws only].** Offline is a backup after a two-minute connection loss and needs a prior login and initial sync; offline card payments on the smart terminal are stored and authorised later, and a decline is the merchant's loss; reviews praise matrices and ease of setup and criticise support, reports and (an older review) loyalty needing a third party.
- **Square [W SQ-price, SQ-ctry; Ws].** Free, Plus (inventory, vendors and purchase orders, cost reports) and Premium (cross-location returns, transfer orders) tiers; **not available in India**; offline payments must be uploaded within 72 hours and the merchant is responsible for expired or declined ones.
- **Copy.** Shopify's reason code for every non-sale cash movement; the card-offline limits idea. **Avoid.** Anything that needs a card network to be present to finish a bill: in India that is every UPI and EMI tender.

### 2.11 Payment-led devices and the simple billing apps

- **Pine Labs Hub [W PL-hub].** A touchscreen terminal with a printer and speaker: billing and inventory, an EMI catalogue across banks, **khata with automated payment reminders**, a customer-facing screen, an online store, and acceptance of cards, UPI, wallets, loyalty rewards, gift cards and DCC; a companion soundbox confirms UPI. Not stated: GST invoicing (only free stand-alone GST tools are mentioned), barcode scanning, offline use, price. The developer page names a POS API suite and an Android library for app-to-app calls [W PL-dev].
- **Razorpay POS [W RZ-docs, RZ-qzap].** All-in-one Android terminals, minis and soundboxes; the **dynamic soundbox is the one the page says works with billing solutions**; Q-Zap handhelds and self-checkout, and a claim of 40% shorter billing time (29 May 2024, unverified).
- **Paytm [Ws].** An Android POS with built-in printer and scanner and GST bills through the Paytm for Business app (launch coverage, Feb 2020), a Business Khata ledger for udhaar, and a soundbox. Specifications are from launch coverage, not current pages.
- **Khatabook, myBillBook, Dukaan [Ws, W DK-home].** Khatabook is a free offline credit ledger; myBillBook is cloud GST billing with e-invoice and e-way bill from a few rupees a month on its own page; Dukaan is an online-store builder with no counter POS on the page I read.
- **What they teach [J].** The udhaar ledger and the reminder are a product by themselves for a kirana (Comsky's bill-wise ledgers are stronger than any of them), the soundbox is the cashier's payment truth in India (and the fraud surface, section 9), and the terminal makers are happy to be integrated by a billing app.

### 3.3 What the matrix says [J]

1. **Nobody has everything, and the cells that are "Y" across the board are the cheap ones**: barcode scan, a few tenders, GST invoice, WhatsApp sharing. The cells that separate a counter system from a billing screen are session and close, hold and recall, returns and exchanges, schemes, loyalty with consent, roles with approvals, and device support. Only GoFrugal, LogicERP, Zoho and Odoo show most of those.
2. **Most "?" cells are in the Indian accounting-first products** (BUSY, Vyapar, Tally). That is where a Tally retailer's current tool is thinnest, and where a ledger-first product with a real counter has room.
3. **Two things only Comsky could do well because it already has them:** one set of books where the POS bill is the accounting voucher (no day-end summary posting to chase), and bill-wise credit for udhaar that a CA can audit. Neither is a feature the matrix has a row for.

## 4. Flow by flow: what the leaders do, what Comsky has, what to do

Each flow has three lines: **Leaders** (evidence-tagged), **Comsky today** (from the code audit unless I read the file myself, then tagged [C]), **Do** (judgement, tagged by version in section 10). Where the batch, serial and label spec already designs the thing, I say "spec" and stop.

### 4.1 The counter screen and billing speed

- **Leaders.** They remove questions: a Tally POS bill can leave the buyer "Not Applicable" and ties payment ledgers to a voucher class [W TL-help]; GoFrugal ships item favourites and item images for touch [W GF-kb]; Zoho shows stock and price under each item tile on its touch screen [W ZP-news]. The speed claims are vendor numbers with no method: "2x faster checkouts" (GoFrugal), "40% less billing time" (Razorpay Q-Zap), "40 to 50% faster" (Marg, from a sibling doc) [W GF-pos, RZ-qzap; R pos-pharma-market §2.2].
- **Comsky today.** A 10-line GST invoice is 115 key actions and four questions (party, place of supply, reference, sales ledger) come before the first item; Ctrl+A accepts but skips the bill-wise panel [R pos-code-audit §1.2].
- **Do.** The audit's answer stands: an **own counter root and writer** that builds the same `Voucher`, with a **terminal profile** (default walk-in party, sales ledger by item group, godown, series, tender ledgers, receipt layout) so nothing is asked before the first item. The date is the device clock, shown, never carried [R pos-code-audit §4].

### 4.2 Item lookup, barcode, and the Alias a Tally retailer already has

- **Leaders.** Tally and BUSY hold the barcode in the item's **Alias** field; BUSY says an unrecognised code raises "Invalid Item" and F4 opens the list; Tally's help gives a 16-digit alias as the example and says labels cannot be printed [W BU-pos, TL-help]. Odoo reads weight, price and discount out of the barcode itself and has **discount tags** [W OD-shop]. Vyapar generates and prints barcodes for loose items and imports items from barcodes [W VY-pos]. LogicERP puts "multiple barcodes per item" in its ₹1,008 tier, which says the need is real [W LG-desktop].
- **Comsky today.** Alias and part number are the only match; a scanner works only if the code was typed into the alias; an unknown scan fails silently and the next scan appends to it; one barcode per item [R pos-code-audit §1.3]. In `main` and not wired: exact-code resolution in the order unit, batch, item or pack or variant, scale code, GS1, alias; the scan-burst detector; GS1 and scale-label parsers [C `src/core/scanResolve.ts`, `scanInput.ts`, `scale.ts`, `barcode/gs1.ts`; spec 14.2 to 14.4].
- **Do (v1).** Wire the **exact-code resolver** into the counter's scan target; **say "not found" and offer Create item or Attach to an item** (the spec's prompt, 14.5); keep alias as the last step so **a Tally retailer's Alias barcodes keep working after the import** (spec 14.4 step 6); multiple and pack barcodes come from the spec's `ItemBarcode`. Label printing from a purchase is the spec's section 13, and it answers the one thing Tally says it cannot do.

### 4.3 Loose, weighed and open items

- **Leaders.** GoFrugal lists weighable-barcode support, scale integration and decimal quantity in all four of its counter products, and **open item** (a one-off line with no master) in three of them [W GF-kb]; Vyapar, Zoho, LogicERP and Odoo name weighing scales [W VY-pos, ZP-feat, LG-pos, OD-pos]. Odoo's barcode nomenclature can carry the weight or the price in the code [W OD-shop].
- **Comsky today.** Units take 0 to 4 decimals and compound units convert on entry; no scale support [R pos-code-audit §1.3]. Scale-label profiles (`21`-prefixed EAN, `W`+item+grams, `U`+item+count, a 16-character weight-and-price form) exist as pure code; **no Indian scale maker's own format was found and the price block's unit is unverified** [C `src/core/scale.ts` header].
- **Do.** v1: weighed items through the scale label read by the scanner (no scale cable); an **open item** only for a role that may, with a reason, a company-set GST rate and HSN, and a line that says "open item" on the bill (otherwise it is a way to dodge a price list and a tax rate). v2: a serial or USB scale read directly.

### 4.4 Hold and recall

- **Leaders.** Marg ("hold a bill and serve the next customer"), GoFrugal (Hold Bill and Recall Bill; held bills list at **that counter only**; no expiry stated), Zoho and Odoo (a plus sign sets an order aside; an Orders button lists them) [W MG-retail, GF-hold, ZP-what, OD-use]. Tally's marketing implies it for repair shops and does not list it [W TL-mkt].
- **Comsky today.** No draft concept; the Optional flag burns a voucher number and Table 13 leaves Optional vouchers out [R pos-code-audit §4, §5].
- **Do (v1).** A device-local drafts table, written on every change, **no number**, no stock reservation; a list with age and cashier; a held bill is re-priced at today's price on recall and any change is shown; open holds are listed at shift close ("held" must not mean "forgotten"). Recall by list; recall by scanning a hold slip is a nice-to-have **[J]**.

### 4.5 Tender, split tender, change, UPI and card

- **Leaders.** Tally: cash with change, plus multi-mode (Alt+I) over gift vouchers, cards and cheques [W TL-help]. BUSY: up to five modes [W BU-pos]. GoFrugal lists cash, credit card, wallet, due bill, credit sale and coupon tenders [W GF-kb]. LogicERP pushes the bill amount to a Pine Labs terminal and takes the status back, Pine Labs and Razorpay sell integration suites for that, and Marg matches UPI and card receipts to bills [W LG-pine, PL-dev, RZ-docs, MG-pos].
- **Comsky today.** No tender list. **A second debit leg on a Sales voucher is counted as taxable turnover** (`voucherGstSummary` adds any non-tax, non-party leg to taxable value), so the audit's shapes are S1 (the party **is** the tender ledger, no engine change) and S2 (the Sales plus a same-transaction Receipt, no engine change); S3 needs a reader audit; S4 makes the cash ledger wrong all day [R pos-code-audit §2.1, §2.3]. The UPI link carries no transaction reference [R pos-code-audit §2.5].
- **Do (v1).** S1 for a single tender, S2 for a mix; keys for exact cash and for a typed tendered amount with the change in large type; a UPI QR with the bill amount (on the screen and the receipt); a **typed reference** per UPI and card tender (UTR tail, approval code, last four digits, never a full number) and a day-end **"unmatched tenders"** list against the imported bank statement, because the cashier's phone or soundbox is not proof [Ws razorpay-learn]. Offline, UPI and card are recorded, not confirmed. The sibling payments study goes further and is the reference for the build: a tender record with a **confirmation state** (confirmed by a PSP, a terminal or the bank statement, or attested by the cashier), the **three card-terminal integration models** (standalone with typed slip data, cloud-integrated, wired ECR) and the behaviours they force (an expiring request, a two-step cancel, a payment taken but not recorded), a **cost-per-tender view** for the 2026 UPI merchant discount and **no UPI surcharge ever**, and **pay first, then issue the invoice with the payment reference printed**, which also satisfies the B2C QR circular [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §0 findings 1, 3 and 4, §1.3, §1.5, §1.8].

### 4.6 Returns and exchanges

- **Leaders.** Odoo: order-based and standalone refunds, and a refund may be issued as a gift card [W OD-use]. LogicERP: "easy exchange and return management" and an alteration module [W LG-kb]. GoFrugal: sales return in all four products, exchange not found [W GF-kb]. Shopify: returns at the original location unless on Pro [W SH-price]. A fraud-control guide advises tying a return to the original tender and transaction and not refunding cash on a card sale [Ws spartanpos].
- **Comsky today.** A Credit Note books stock back and nets into GSTR-1 correctly, but a cash bill has no bill reference, so a return cannot be tied to its bill and nothing stops returning more than was sold [R pos-code-audit §5].
- **Do (v1).** Store the original voucher id on the bill and the returnable quantity per line; **print the bill number as a barcode or QR on the receipt and scan it to open the return** **[J]**; refund to the original tender by default, cash refunds capped and needing the manager's PIN; an **exchange is a Credit Note plus a Sale in one transaction with one net tender**; the returned unit goes back at its original cost; a return that crosses a financial year gets a warning: section 34 of the Act ties a credit note to a cut-off after the year of the supply; **my copy (1 Aug 2021) says September, and the sibling study, reading the CBIC rules copy and a search summary, gives 30 November following the year, by the Finance Act 2022 from 1 Oct 2022** (consistent with my recollection, not confirmed by me); a credit note names the original invoice's serial and date (Rule 53(1A), also from the sibling) [W CGST-act s.34; R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §3.1, §1.7].

### 4.7 Discounts, promotions, coupons and schemes

- **Leaders.** Odoo: global and line discounts, promotions that apply on a minimum spend, codes and coupons, buy-X-get-Y added **by the cashier by hand**, discount tags [W OD-pricing, OD-shop]. Marg: slab-wise discounts, schemes, vouchers [W MG-pos]. BUSY: compound discounts, scheme management, "Buy 1 Get 1" and "20% off MRP" examples [W BU-retail, BU-vs]. GoFrugal: customer-type discount by amount slab and date (2017), a cap on cash discount, "offers and promotions" marked coming soon on the pricing page [W GF-rel, GF-price]. Tally: a discount column, free items, and BOGO through separate Actual and Billed quantity columns [W TL-help].
- **The law the engine must follow [W C92, CGST-act s.15(3)].** A discount recorded on the invoice at or before supply is out of the value of supply, including a "buy more, save more" slab; a post-supply discount is out of value only if agreed at or before the supply, linked to the invoices and the recipient reverses the credit; a later "secondary" discount by credit note is a commercial matter and **does not reduce the taxable value**; **buy-one-get-one is two supplies at one price**, with the rate set by the composite or mixed supply rule, and input credit stays; goods given free with **no consideration** are not a supply and the credit on them is blocked.
- **Comsky today.** A line discount exists only when F11 "Use discount column" is on and clamps 0 to 100; no bill-level discount, coupon or scheme; **a Discount ledger in the extras sits outside the taxable value**, so the customer would be charged tax on the pre-discount figure; a free line (amount 0) cannot be entered [R pos-code-audit §1.4]. `allocate` (largest remainder, exact to the paisa) exists for the split [R pos-code-audit §1.4, `gst.ts`].
- **Do.** v1: line and bill discount by percent or rupee, **allocated across the lines before tax**; a cap per role and a reason code; the manager's PIN above the cap; a free line as a zero-priced line with a reason and a log entry. v2: a **rules engine as data** (bundle, slab, combo, time window, customer group) that emits discount lines allocated before tax; a BOGO is a bundle priced once, never a "free" line; a gift with no consideration is flagged for the credit-block note. The slab test of section 6 uses the **net unit price after an on-invoice discount** [Ws accountune].

### 4.8 Loyalty, wallet and gift cards

- **Leaders.** Vyapar, Marg, LogicERP, GoFrugal, Odoo and Pine Labs Hub all list points; Marg adds welcome offers; **LogicERP asks an OTP before redeeming points or a coupon**; Odoo sells gift cards at the register, keeps an eWallet and needs the customer chosen first [W VY-pos, MG-pos, LG-pos, OD-pricing, PL-hub]. GoFrugal's loyalty is missing from its mobile product [W GF-kb].
- **Comsky today.** Nothing; the CRM tables are a sales pipeline, not a customer master [R pos-code-audit §1.6].
- **Do.** Not v1. v2: a light **customer table** (phone first, name, consent, points, wallet) with a ledger only for credit customers; points with expiry; redemption confirmed by a short code; a gift card or wallet as a liability ledger (the sibling study reads, through commentary, CBIC Circular 243/37/2024-GST of 31 Dec 2024: a voucher's sale is an actionable claim, the underlying supply is taxed when the voucher is redeemed, breakage on unredeemed vouchers is untaxed; a CA must confirm [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §1.7]). **Collecting a phone number is personal data**: the DPDP Rules were notified in mid-November 2025 (13 Nov in the search summaries I saw, 14 Nov in the PIB note the sibling study read) with an 18-month phase-in, so the main duties bite from about mid-May 2027; the consent line, purpose and withdrawal belong in the first customer form, and "to send you this bill" and "to send you offers" are two consents [Ws azbpartners, hoganlovells; R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §4.5]. Keep marketing messages separate from the bill message.

### 4.9 Credit sales (udhaar) and collecting dues

- **Leaders.** Pine Labs Hub digitises credit records with automated reminders; Paytm has a Business Khata; Vyapar sends payment reminders; GoFrugal has credit-sale and due-bill tenders [W PL-hub, VY-home, GF-kb; Ws Paytm].
- **Comsky today.** The bill-wise engine is complete (New Ref named after the bill, receipts against bills, credit limit with a reason), but a credit sale saved with Ctrl+A skips the bill panel and reads as On Account, and collecting needs a full Receipt with Agst Ref [R pos-code-audit §1.2, §1.6].
- **Do (v1).** Credit is a tender whose party is the customer's ledger (S1), so the New Ref is made at the counter; **"Collect dues"** from the counter takes cash or UPI against named bills; a reminder by `wa.me`; a credit limit with a manager override; the customer list used for credit is the small set of ledgers, not every shopper.

### 4.10 Estimates, challans and delivery bills

- **Leaders.** Vyapar converts estimates to invoices and has delivery challans; Odoo loads quotations and sales orders into the register with a down payment and has "ship later"; GoFrugal has delivery bill, delivery note and sales order [W VY-home, OD-shop, GF-kb, GF-hold].
- **Comsky today.** Sales Order and Delivery Note voucher types exist [C `docs/FEATURE-PARITY.md`]; nothing from the counter.
- **Do.** v2: open an order or estimate at the counter and bill part or all; a **delivery bill** flag that needs the customer and address; a down payment as a receipt against the order.

### 4.11 Price lists, MRP and value-slab rates

- **Leaders.** GoFrugal: price levels, location-wise selling prices, tax-slab support [W GF-kb, GF-pos]; LogicERP: several price lists at its middle tier [W LG-desktop]; Vyapar: billing at MRP with the tax inside it [W VY-home]; Zoho is "preparing for" multiple selling prices and MRPs per batch [W ZP-ios].
- **Comsky today.** Price levels exist (Alt+Z) [R pos-code-audit §1.3]; rates are tax-exclusive and held to two decimals, so a basket of MRP-priced goods cannot be made to equal the sum of MRPs by construction; the GST rate is chosen by day (`gstRates.ts`) and **has no value rule** [R pos-code-audit §1.3; C `src/core/gstRates.ts`].
- **Do (v1).** An **MRP-inclusive mode** (the line total is fixed, the tax is derived, the exclusive rate is stored with extra precision); **a hard stop above MRP for a packaged good** with a manager override and reason (Rule 18(2) bars a sale above the retail price) [W LM]; a **value-slab rate** held as data on the item or group (up to a ceiling the rate is one number, above it another) evaluated on the net unit price at save, with the rate shown on the line; the basis and multi-piece behaviour signed by the CA (section 6).

### 4.12 Cashier, salesperson, rights and approvals

- **Leaders.** Odoo's three tiers (minimal, basic, advanced) with login by badge, name or PIN; Zoho's role-based permissions; Marg's user-specific drawer permissions; Shopify's roles on Pro; GoFrugal's salesman commission; Vyapar's roles are predefined only, and a reviewer wants custom ones [W OD-emp, ZP-home, MG-pos, SH-price, GF-kb, VY-tp].
- **Comsky today.** Rights are client-side and coarse; a cashier needs the Accounting area, which also opens the Day Book and cash book; there is no cancel, discount, override, refund, reprint or no-sale right; the server accepts any payload from any member; a PIN check costs 260 ms on the HTTP site [R pos-code-audit §9].
- **Do (v1).** New rights (discount cap, price override, void, refund, no-sale, reprint, close shift, open item) with a **manager PIN approval** that records who approved, and a cashier switch in a couple of seconds (a short device PIN after the person has signed in). Salesperson per bill and per line as a field of the bill. Enforcement on the server is the audit's lane F and v2; until then every approval and every refused attempt is an event.

### 4.13 Cash drawer, shift, Z report and day end

- **Leaders.** The same pattern in Odoo, Zoho and Shopify: open with a float; cash in and out with a reason; at close, expected cash by method against a counted figure, a difference that can be limited and sent to a manager; a printed summary [W OD-use, ZE-sess; Ws Shopify]. LogicERP and GoFrugal have a cashier shift system; GoFrugal prints a session summary [W LG-kb, GF-kb].
- **Comsky today.** No shift, float, count by denomination, variance ledger, blind close, Z report or no-sale record [R pos-code-audit §2.4].
- **Do (v1).** As the audit sketches: one cash ledger per terminal; a shift record (open float, in and out with reasons, expected, counted by denomination, over or short posted to a "Cash short and excess" ledger); a **blind count** (the screen does not show expected until counted); a **maximum difference** above which a manager must approve (Odoo's rule); a Z report and a tender summary; a close that warns about open holds. An open shift left behind by a crashed device needs a **remote close by the manager** (Shopify's community reports stale sessions) [Ws].

### 4.14 Receipt, customer display and e-bill

- **Leaders.** Thermal slips of 2, 3 and 4 inches [W VY-pos, BU-pos]; the cash drawer opens on a cash sale [W VY-pos]; pole displays and customer screens [W GF-kb, ZP-feat, PL-hub, OD-pos]; WhatsApp at the time of billing [W MG-pos, VY-home].
- **Comsky today.** Every receipt goes through `window.print()` on A4, A5 or Letter; no silent print, drawer or kiosk; the WhatsApp link is a letter of text [R pos-code-audit §8].
- **Do.** v1: 58 and 80 mm receipts (silent, through a validated Electron bridge in the style of `exportBridge.cjs`), a drawer kick through the printer, a DUPLICATE mark and a reprint count, tax by rate, an optional UPI QR, and share by `wa.me` with a PDF the person can attach. v2: a second window as customer display; a provider-based WhatsApp e-bill (section 6 lists the cost and the template step). The sibling study is the reference for how a receipt reaches a printer: a hybrid of native ESC/POS text and raster lines (the rupee sign and Indian scripts are not reliably in a thermal printer's code pages), the drawer pulse through the printer, the customer display and a serial scale, and the point that **only the Windows Electron app reaches every device without extra software** while a browser needs HTTPS and a chooser per device [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §0 findings 11 to 13, §2.2 to §2.6]. A hosted e-bill page with a link is a third e-bill kind that needs only our backend over HTTPS (§4.1 there).

### 4.15 GST at the counter

- **Do (v1), by case.** B2C is the default; **entering a GSTIN makes it a B2B tax invoice** with the buyer's details; for an unregistered buyer at Rs 50,000 or more the bill needs name, address, delivery address and state with code (Rule 46(e)); a **composition** or exempt counter issues a **bill of supply** that shows no tax (Rule 49, section 31(3)(c); the sibling study adds that a composition dealer's bill carries a fixed legend, "composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies", Rule 5(1)(f)); **a basket that mixes taxable and exempt goods (a grocery counter's milk and vegetables beside packaged goods) is one invoice-cum-bill of supply under Rule 46A**, which I could not read myself (CBIC's page for it returned an error) and take from the sibling study [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §0 finding 7, §3.1]; HSN digits follow the company's turnover band; the e-invoice for a B2B bill of a company above the threshold **cannot be completed at the counter in v1** because the app never generates an IRN [C CLAUDE.md]; v2 uses the GSP path in `einvoice-gsp.md`. Credit notes for returns follow the original [R pos-code-audit §1.5]. Details and sources are in section 6.

### 4.16 Multi-counter, multi-store, central pricing and transfers

- **Leaders.** GoFrugal: location-wise selling prices, per-store GST structures, store-to-store transactions, a security code on a transfer in; Marg: compare stock and sales across stores and move stock with a record; LogicERP: multi-location at its top edition; Zoho: transfer orders and per-location pricing; Shopify: Pro per location; Square: transfer orders on Premium [W GF-pos, GF-rel, MG-retail, LG-desktop, ZP-feat, SH-price, SQ-price].
- **Comsky today.** A company is one GSTIN; a godown is a location inside it; no outlet dimension on a voucher; no consolidated multi-company report; a device pulls the whole tenant [R pos-code-audit §10.1, §3.3].
- **Do.** v1: several counters of **one store** in one company, each with its own series and cash ledger. v2: several outlets in one state as godown plus series plus cash ledger each, transfers through a Stock Journal with a transit godown (spec 3.5), a central item and price list pushed as a price level per outlet, and reports by outlet through the custom-field tag (a label that never reaches a GST return) [R pos-code-audit §10.1]. v3: several GSTINs and consolidation.

### 4.17 Hardware and platforms

- **Leaders.** Vyapar names USB and Bluetooth scanners, thermal printers at 2, 3 and 4 inches, a cash drawer and a USB or Bluetooth scale; Marg tells buyers to confirm which counters and devices work before buying; Odoo reaches printers, displays and scales through an IoT connection; Pine Labs and Razorpay terminals are Android [W VY-pos, MG-pos, OD-pos, PL-hub, RZ-docs].
- **Comsky today.** Windows and Mac desktop shell; no printer list, silent print, kiosk or single-instance lock; the live site is plain HTTP, which blocks the camera, service workers and install [R pos-code-audit §7].
- **Do.** Windows first (low-end PCs are normal [J]); one **device bridge** (silent print to a named printer, printer list, drawer, kiosk window, single instance), each call a pure validator; a short **"hardware we tested"** list in the product, and nothing promised beyond it, because no hardware was touched in this study. Android tablet after HTTPS. The device paths, the ESC/POS emitter and the bench list the owner can run before anything is sized are in the sibling study [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §2.5 to §2.7].

### 4.18 Offline, power cuts, peaks and aggregators

- **Leaders.** Odoo's cart survives an outage but not a browser reload; Marg's own help describes database damage after a power cut; GoFrugal sells GoBill to cut queues at peak hours; Odoo lets an operator choose how many products and customers load into the offline cache [W OD-use, MG-retail; R pos-pharma-market §2.2]. For online and aggregator orders: GoFrugal lists ONDC, Unicommerce and Shopify, Marg records phone and online orders, Odoo reaches online delivery platforms through UrbanPiper in its restaurant mode [W GF-home, MG-retail, OD-pos]; the restaurant doc covers the order-in rules [R pos-restaurant-market §3.7].
- **Comsky today.** Local-first books in IndexedDB are the right base; the risks are the audit's lane A (per-keystroke and per-bill work that grows with the book, a full reload per pulled page, backups that stop near 290,000 bills) [R pos-code-audit §3].
- **Do.** The cart draft is written on every change (a power cut returns the cart); numbering is device-owned (audit §4); a festival day at five times the average is a bill every two seconds at 3,000 a day, so the hot path is a precondition, and a **second billing device on the same store** (a phone or tablet counter) is the queue breaker. Aggregators and ONDC are v3 for retail.

## 5. The ten things that make a counter POS fast

"Billing in under 10 seconds" needs a definition before it is a target. **[J, with unverified assumptions]** A trained cashier scans about one item every 1.2 seconds; a 5-item bill is about 6 seconds of scanning, 2 seconds to tender (one key and, for cash, one amount) and about 1 second from Accept to the bytes reaching the printer: **9 seconds, and the software's share must stay under about 1 second for the whole bill** (150 ms per scan, 300 ms from Accept to print). A 40-item trolley is a minute whatever the software does; what the software controls is that **nothing stalls between scans**. The vendors' own claims (2x, 40%, 40 to 50%) have no stated method, so the tests below are the ones I would hold Comsky to.

| # | The thing | Evidence | Test we can run | Comsky today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Zero questions before the first item.** Defaults from a terminal profile: walk-in party, sales ledger, godown, series, date = today (shown). | Tally lets the buyer be "Not Applicable" [W TL-help]; GoFrugal calls it one-touch billing [W GF-pos, claim] | From a new bill to the first line: a scan only, no key before it | Four questions first [R pos-code-audit §1.2] |
| 2 | **Scan-first focus that never leaves.** After every add the cursor is back on the scan target; no modal for tax, rate or quantity unless the item needs one. | Odoo, BUSY barcode flows; spec 14.3 scan target | 20 scans in a row with no mouse and no lost focus; a scan that lands anywhere else is said and swallowed (spec 14.3) | The Sales screen is a field-by-field walk; scan layer not wired |
| 3 | **Search by anything, exact code first.** Barcode, pack code, alias, part number, short code, name, brand; phone for a customer; bill number for a return. An unknown code is said, with Create or Attach. | BUSY "Invalid Item" and F4; Tally alias; Marg's search list in `pos-pharma-market.md`; spec 14.4 | Exact code in under 50 ms at 30,000 items before any substring filter; 0 silent failures | Alias and part number only, silent miss [R pos-code-audit §1.3] |
| 4 | **Quantity and unit by keystroke.** A repeat scan adds one; a multiplier then a scan (`3` then scan) sets the quantity; the last line is nudged with arrows; weighed items take the scale label; default quantity 1. | GoFrugal lists default quantity, decimal quantity and weighable barcodes [W GF-kb]; Odoo's numpad modes [W OD-use] | A 12-item basket of mixed counts in 25 key actions, 0 mouse | Quantity is a typed field per line; unit suffix typing exists [R pos-code-audit §1.3] |
| 5 | **Price and tax already right at the scan.** MRP-inclusive, price level, dated rate, value-slab rate, HSN, cess: nothing asked. | GoFrugal "tax slab support", Vyapar MRP billing [W GF-kb, VY-home]; section 6 | 10,000 random baskets: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and HSN equal the Sales-screen build to the paisa (the audit's lane B exit) | Rates entered by hand per bill; no MRP-inclusive; no value rule [R pos-code-audit §1.1, §1.3] |
| 6 | **One key per tender; exact cash first.** F-key per tender, an "exact" key, a numpad for the tendered amount, change in large type, a split is repeating the tender. | Tally Alt+I; BUSY five modes; Odoo's payment screen [W TL-help, BU-pos, OD-use] | A single-item cash bill is a scan and one key: 2 actions | No tender concept [R pos-code-audit §2] |
| 7 | **Print, drawer and WhatsApp are side effects of the tender, not steps.** Silent print on Accept, a drawer kick, an optional phone number asked once, and the receipt exists **only after** the tender is recorded. | Vyapar's drawer opens on cash [W VY-pos]; a Vyapar reviewer's WhatsApp-before-payment complaint [W VY-tp] | Accept to bytes at the printer in 300 ms or less (spool time, not paper); no dialog | `window.print()` dialog: three or more actions [R pos-code-audit §8] |
| 8 | **Hold and recall in one key each, and a hold costs nothing.** No number, no stock reservation, a list with age. | Marg, GoFrugal, Zoho, Odoo [W] | Hold, serve another, recall: 3 actions and the cart is exact | None; Optional burns a number [R pos-code-audit §5] |
| 9 | **Local first: nothing per scan or per bill grows with the book or needs the network.** Device-owned numbering, a local stock check, background sync. | The audit's measurements: 66 ms a keystroke at 120,000 vouchers, 223 ms to Accept at 250,000 [R pos-code-audit §3.2] | Scan and Accept flat within noise at 250,000 and 1,100,000 synthetic vouchers on the perf harness | O(N) per keystroke and per save [R pos-code-audit §3.2] |
| 10 | **Survive the shift: a crash-proof cart, touch and keyboard parity, and a cashier who has never seen it.** The draft is written on every change; item tiles with image and favourites; regional-language labels; a PIN switch in about 2 seconds. | Marg's database-damage pages and multilingual invoices; GoFrugal favourites and images; Zoho's 13 languages [R pos-pharma-market §2.2; W GF-kb, MG-pos, ZP-what] | Kill the process mid-bill: the cart returns with the same lines; a first-time user bills a 5-item basket from the tile grid without help | No draft; the shell has a 938 px floor and no touch layout [R pos-code-audit §7] |

**Also worth having, and cheap [J]:** a held "next customer" indicator, big-key mode for a tablet, a sound for a bad scan (the spec's 14.6), a "last bill" reprint key, and the bill number printed as a code so a return starts with a scan.

## 10. Recommendation **[J]**

### 10.1 Positioning

- **Who.** A small multi-counter retailer (one to three counters, a few thousand SKUs, a few cashiers) whose CA uses Tally or Comsky: general retail, supermarket, mobile accessories, stationery and gifts first; apparel and electronics next. Not a kirana-first product (that is a war with ₹700 a year apps and free ledger apps), not a chain product (that needs multi-store and consolidation first), not restaurants (its own doc).
- **The promise.** **"The counter that posts to the same books."** A POS bill is the accounting voucher, so GST, stock, cash, credit and the CA's reports agree by construction. The proof points are the ones competitors skip or get wrong: GST correct on discounts, schemes and price slabs; MRP-inclusive billing with a hard stop above MRP; a shift close with a blind count; detective controls on by default; keyboard-first speed; Tally XML in and out.
- **Packaging.** One published price per company, no per-terminal or per-user fee, every counter feature included; add-ons only where a third party charges (WhatsApp messages, a terminal partner). Reference points: BUSY ₹5,000 to ₹12,000 a year, Marg ₹5,550 to ₹26,000 plus GST, GoFrugal "from ₹750 a month" per store, LogicERP ₹608 to ₹6,154 a month, Zoho $20 to $75 per location a month, Shopify POS Pro ₹7,000 a month per location [W, section 1].
- **Channel.** The owner's Tally reseller network: installation, hardware advice, training and local support, which is the loudest unmet need in the field (section 7).
- **Do not claim** "AI-powered", a fiscal or tax-authority endorsement, or that a bill is "sent" when the app only opened a link (CLAUDE.md rules).

### 10.2 The shape of the counter screen (a contract for lane D, not a design)

One root that replaces the shell on a terminal in POS mode: a cart (left), a search and tile area (right) that is the scan target by default, totals and tender keys (bottom), a header with customer, price level, hold, recall, return and the shift and sync state, and the existing F-key rail kept for the keyboard-first person. Targets: 44 px minimum for anything touched, light and dark, compact and comfortable, 150% text size, 1024 by 768 and a 1280 by 800 tablet, every key from `core/shortcutRegistry.ts`, nothing new bound outside it [R pos-code-audit §7, §14].

### 10.4 What not to build

An own card terminal or payment gateway (the terminal makers want to be integrated); AI features or "AI accounting" (the product contains none, CLAUDE.md); a fiscal-printer path (not an Indian requirement I found); direct marketplace APIs before the partner programmes exist (the restaurant doc's finding for aggregators); a free-form promotion scripting language (rules as data with a fixed set of shapes); a second copy of GST logic (the POS writer builds the same `Voucher` and the engines stay the only taxers).

### 10.5 Exit criteria for v1 (pilot-ready)

1. A single scanned item with exact cash is **two actions**; a 10-line basket with a split tender is a scan per line plus about five keys; Enter-to-print is under 300 ms at 250,000 vouchers on the perf harness, with no per-scan growth (section 5).
2. GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, the HSN table and Table 13 equal the Sales-screen build for 10,000 random baskets and tender mixes, to the paisa; the value-slab rule, the bill-level discount split and an MRP basket each have a golden.
3. A killed process mid-bill returns the cart; a power cut loses no saved bill; a restore of a day's backup has been run (restore has never been tested [C CLAUDE.md]).
4. A shift closes with a blind count, a difference above the limit waits for a manager, and the Z report ties to the cash ledger.
5. Every void, discount over cap, override, refund, no-sale and reprint is an event with operator, terminal and time, and the exceptions list shows them per cashier.
6. A real 58 mm and a real 80 mm printer, a USB scanner, a drawer and a scale label were used by someone, on a packaged build, on Windows 10 and 11 (nothing in this study was).
7. One pilot retailer with a Tally company: Alias barcodes and masters imported, a week of real bills, the CA's GSTR-1 compared with their usual one.

### 10.6 Sequencing against the other work

- **One scan layer, wired once.** The batch and serial spec owns the scan detector, resolver and label engines (T22, T25, T35); the counter is their first big consumer. The wiring (a scan target, the not-found prompt, the burst observer) should be one slice shared by the counter and the unit grid, not two.
- **One Dexie bump, one editor of `db.ts`.** The audit's lane C needs additive tables (shifts, drafts, customers, perhaps detail bills); the spec may add `labelTemplates`; the custom-fields build avoids a bump. Decide the single version before anyone writes tables [R pos-code-audit §12].
- **Variants and serials before apparel and electronics.** CF-9 (stock by variant, pooled costing per item) is built and unreviewed in the worktree; the spec's serial and IMEI rules are designed and unbuilt [R spec; C `.wt-cf/docs/design/custom-fields-spec.md` CF24 to CF26].
- **The restaurant and pharmacy docs share the root, the tender, shift and rights.** Build those once under lanes B to D; the vertical packs (lane H) differ by items, tables, KOT, batch and Schedule H1 records.
- **The receipt bridge copies the label bridge.** `electron/labelBridge.cjs` and `labelLang.cjs` emit a printer language from a structured job so a hostile page can never reach a printer command; the receipt path adds an ESC/POS emitter and a job validator of the same shape, never bytes from the renderer [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §0 finding 13, §2.6]. Build it once with the label work.
- **HTTPS and the Android shell** wait on the hosting decision (mobile docs); the desktop pilot does not. **TLS and a hostname come before the first PSP or WhatsApp provider is called** [R pos-payments-hardware-compliance §0 finding 14].
- **The installers must be rebuilt** for any device bridge; the PDF bridge from wave 3 has never run on a real Electron binary [C CLAUDE.md].

## 11. Risks, open questions and what to verify

### 11.1 Risks, in order

1. **The hot path and sync (the audit's risks 1, 3, 6).** Everything above is slower than a Marg or a GoFrugal on a low-end PC until lane A lands. v1 does not start its screen work before it.
2. **Hardware nobody has touched.** Silent print, drawer kick, scanner timing and a scale label are all untested here; the first real printer is the first real risk **[J]**.
3. **GST readings.** The value-slab basis, the bill-level discount split, BOGO and gift cards are my reading of rules; a wrong default is a statutory error on every bill. A CA signs first (D7).
4. **Payment-partner dependency.** The terminal integration is the part of the market (Pine Labs, Razorpay) that sets the pace, and neither developer flow was readable.
5. **Support load.** The field's top complaint is also what a small team cannot scale; the reseller channel is the plan and it is a plan, not a fact.
6. **Zoho.** A Zoho-owned retail ERP plus POS plus Books with Indian GST is the strongest direct rival for "books and POS together".
7. **Scope.** Loyalty, promotions, multi-store and variants each look like "one more feature" and are not; v1 holds the line at the single-store counter.
8. **Customer data.** A phone number at the counter is the start of personal-data duties from 2027 on the weight of sources.

### 11.2 Questions for the owner and the CA

1. The three numbers from the audit: bills per day per outlet, B2B share, credit share.
2. How many of the owner's Tally customers are retailers, what do they use at the counter today, and would two or three pilot?
3. Printers and scanners the reseller already sells or supports: use those first.
4. CA: the basis of "sale value" for the Rs 2,500 test (tax-exclusive? after discount? per piece on a multi-piece invoice?); the bill-level discount split; BOGO as a bundle; the GST timing of gift cards and wallet top-ups; whether a company may use the consolidated small-bill route at all in its case; composition counters and the Rs 1.5 crore lapse.
5. An order deposit or booking advance is a receipt voucher under section 31(3)(d) (read in the Act copy); for a **gift voucher** the sibling study reads CBIC Circular 243/37/2024-GST through commentary as "not a supply when sold, taxed when redeemed", which a CA must confirm for this product's flow **[unverified]**.
6. Price: a published per-company price, and what is in it.
7. Which terminal partner to approach first.

### 11.3 What to test or read next

- **Use the products.** Install trials of Vyapar, GoFrugal, Marg and Odoo (all offer a trial or a free tier) and count the key actions for a 10-line basket, a split tender, a hold and a return. I used none.
- **Tally.** Enter a multi-mode POS bill in a real TallyPrime and read the vouchers it posts (the help is silent on the postings) [R pos-code-audit §2.2].
- **Developer docs under NDA:** Pine Labs and Razorpay (the flow of an amount and a status).
- **A day with a real counter** to measure the actual scan gap of the owner's scanner and the print time of its printer.
- **Five retailer interviews** on bills per day, tender mix, holds, returns share, discount habits, theft stories and what the counter does at Diwali.
- **Reviews I could not read:** Capterra, G2, GetApp, the Play Store (Vyapar, GoFrugal, Marg), Reddit and YouTube, and Lightspeed's own pages.
- **Statute:** the primary text of the e-invoice, QR, composition and cash-receipt provisions, and CBIC's own words on "sale value".
